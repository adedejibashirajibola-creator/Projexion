Why we exist

Every mid-market GC has already invested in good software: Procore for the field, Sage for accounting, P6 for scheduling. And yet the answers executives and PMs need still live across 10+ disconnected systems and a wall of spreadsheets.

The pain isn't a lack of software. It's that the software doesn't talk to each other, and turning it into decisions is a full-time data-engineering job construction firms were never built to own. So we built the intelligence layer that does it for them.
